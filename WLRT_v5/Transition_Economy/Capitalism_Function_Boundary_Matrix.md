# Capitalism: Function–Boundary Matrix

**Status:** Developing

## Method

Each mechanism is examined through:

Function
→ Benefit
→ Fragility
→ Boundary
→ Replacement

Replacement means preservation of function through transformation
where possible, not automatic elimination of the mechanism.

---

## 1. Price

**Function**  
Information compression and coordination.

**Benefit**  
Allows heterogeneous resources and preferences to be compared through
a common coordinate.

**Fragility**  
Multidimensional system state is compressed into a signal that does
not necessarily represent fragility, reversibility, redistribution
capability, or loss of future trajectories.

**Boundary**  
Price becomes insufficient when decisions that are locally rational
according to price systematically degrade wider system state.

**Potential Replacement / Extension**

Price
→ Price + State Diagnostics
→ State-Constrained Decisions

**Status:** HYPOTHESIS / DEVELOPING

---

## 2. Money

**Function**  
Universal exchange, coordination, access routing, and temporal
transfer.

**Benefit**  
Reduces coordination complexity across heterogeneous agents.

**Fragility**  
Access to real resources becomes strongly dependent on access to the
monetary layer.

**Boundary**  
To be investigated.

**Potential Replacement / Extension**  
Hybrid monetary and non-monetary access mechanisms.

**Status:** DEVELOPING

---

## 3. Capital

**Function**  
Resource accumulation and intertemporal allocation.

**Benefit**  
Allows resources to be concentrated for productive investment and
large-scale projects.

**Fragility**  
The reproduction of capital may become a goal independent of the
Redistribution Capability of the underlying system.

**Boundary**  
To be investigated.

**Potential Replacement / Extension**  
To be investigated.

---

## 4. Profit

**Function**  
Performance signal and incentive.

**Benefit**  
Provides feedback about local economic outcomes.

**Fragility**  
Local profitability may coexist with transferred or accumulated
systemic fragility.

**Boundary**  
To be investigated.

**Potential Replacement / Extension**  
Profit supplemented by state and trajectory diagnostics.

---

## 5. Ownership

**Function**  
Control, responsibility, incentive, and allocation rights.

**Benefit**  
Creates durable claims and supports long-term decision-making.

**Fragility**  
Ownership can become separated from participation, consequences, and
systemic risk.

**Boundary**  
To be investigated.

---

## 6. Credit

**Function**  
Transfers future economic capacity into the present.

**Benefit**  
Accelerates investment and development.

**Fragility**  
Future states become constrained by obligations created in earlier
states.

**Boundary**  
To be investigated.

---

## 7. Labour

**Function**  
Human participation in production and primary distribution of income.

**Benefit**  
Historically links contribution, income, and resource access.

**Fragility**  
Automation may weaken the link between human labour and production
without removing the link between labour and income.

**Boundary**  
To be investigated.

---

## 8. Competition

**Function**  
Discovery, selection, adaptation, and efficiency pressure.

**Benefit**  
Encourages experimentation and removes persistently inefficient
structures.

**Fragility**  
Efficiency pressure may remove redundancy required for resilience.

**Boundary**  
To be investigated.

---

## 9. Bankruptcy

**Function**  
Local termination of failed economic structures.

**Benefit**  
Releases resources and prevents indefinite accumulation of local
failure.

**Fragility**  
Systemically important entities may become too interconnected to fail
locally.

**Boundary**  
When local failure threatens system-level continuity.

---

## 10. State Stabilization

**Function**  
Systemic buffering, redistribution, regulation, and crisis response.

**Benefit**  
Allows local failures and social shocks to be absorbed.

**Fragility**  
Fragility can be transferred from private/local systems to the
sovereign/macroeconomic level.

**Boundary**  
To be investigated.

---

## 11. Growth

**Function**  
Expansion of production, opportunity, and available economic space.

**Benefit**  
Historically allows conflicts over distribution to be softened through
expansion.

**Fragility**  
Continuous expansion may encounter physical, ecological, demographic,
or systemic boundaries.

**Boundary**  
To be investigated.

---

## 12. Financial Markets

**Function**  
Capital allocation, liquidity provision, price discovery, and risk
transfer.

**Benefit**  
Rapid redistribution of financial resources.

**Fragility**  
Financial liquidity may become partially detached from the
Redistribution Capability of underlying real systems.

**Boundary**  
To be investigated.
