# Research Notes — WLRT Transition Economy

This file preserves observations, hypotheses, questions, and emerging
ideas before they are promoted into the main research map.

---

## Status Definitions

**OBSERVATION**  
An identified phenomenon or conceptual relationship.

**HYPOTHESIS**  
A proposition requiring further development or testing.

**DERIVED**  
A result that can be consistently derived from an established WLRT
framework.

**CANON**  
A proposition deliberately accepted into the architecture of this
research branch.

---

## CANON-01 — Research Method

Analyze economic mechanisms through:

Function
→ Benefit
→ Fragility
→ Boundary
→ Replacement

The objective is to preserve useful functions rather than simply
eliminate existing institutions.

---

## HYPOTHESIS-01 — Money as Complexity Compression

Money functions partly as a mechanism for compressing the complexity
of coordination among heterogeneous agents.

---

## HYPOTHESIS-02 — Price Is Not State

Price is a coordinate or projection of system state but is not a
complete representation of that state.

P ≠ State

---

## HYPOTHESIS-03 — Capital Accumulation vs Redistribution Capability

Local maximization of capital accumulation does not necessarily
maximize the Redistribution Capability of the wider system.

---

## HYPOTHESIS-04 — Functional Displacement

A transition beyond capital-centered economic organization may occur
through functional displacement rather than destruction of existing
institutions.

Money, markets, ownership, and profit may remain while losing their
role as the dominant architecture of economic coordination.

---

## HYPOTHESIS-05 — Redistribution-Capability Economy

A possible successor architecture may organize major economic
decisions around the preservation and development of Redistribution
Capability.

This is currently a research hypothesis, not a canonical WLRT claim.

---

## HYPOTHESIS-06 — Price Transition

A possible early transition sequence is:

Price Dominance
→ Price + State Diagnostics
→ State-Constrained Decisions

---

## HYPOTHESIS-07 — Access as a Fundamental Variable

A fundamental question for future economic architecture may be:

> What constitutes the basis of access to resources?

Possible answers may include combinations of:

- money;
- labour;
- ownership;
- participation;
- need;
- membership;
- contribution to systemic stability.

---

## HYPOTHESIS-08 — Labour–Production Decoupling

AI and automation may increasingly weaken:

Human Labour ↔ Production

while contemporary institutions continue to rely on:

Human Labour ↔ Income ↔ Access

This may create a structural mismatch between production and
distribution.

---

## HYPOTHESIS-09 — Moneyless Subsystems

Strictly moneyless economic mechanisms may be viable as local or
functional subsystems within a larger hybrid economy.

They are not currently treated as the primary candidate for a fully
global economic architecture.

---

## HYPOTHESIS-10 — Phase Transition Rather Than Institutional Destruction

The movement beyond capital dominance may resemble a phase transition:

the components may remain present while their relationships and
dominant functions change.

---

## OBSERVATION-01 — Ownership, Participation, and Control

Contemporary systems increasingly separate:

Ownership
≠ Participation
≠ Control
≠ Carrier of Fragility

The consequences of this separation require further investigation.

---

## OBSERVATION-02 — Asynchronous Economic Time

Financial markets, businesses, institutions, infrastructure, and
social systems operate at different effective phase-time scales.

This may create persistent delays between:

signal
→ recognition
→ intervention
→ consequence.

---

## OPEN QUESTION-01

Can Redistribution Capability be measured sufficiently well to affect
real economic decisions?

## OPEN QUESTION-02

Can LFI or related fragility diagnostics operate at firm, sector,
national, and global scales without losing meaning?

## OPEN QUESTION-03

Which functions of money are genuinely irreplaceable at global scale?

## OPEN QUESTION-04

Can income be partially separated from labour without creating a new
form of structural coercion?

## OPEN QUESTION-05

How can decentralized discovery and entrepreneurial experimentation be
preserved in a state-aware economic architecture?

## OPEN QUESTION-06

Who determines the admissible state constraints?

## OPEN QUESTION-07

How can state-aware coordination avoid becoming centralized planning?

## OPEN QUESTION-08

Can AI reduce coordination complexity enough to change the historical
need for universal price signals?

## OPEN QUESTION-09

What are the phase-transition criteria between a capital-dominant and
a Redistribution-Capability-dominant system?
